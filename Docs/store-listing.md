## Notes

- **Search terms in titles:** "idle", "space", "miner" and "tycoon" are the strongest terms, so they're in the name. Google Play also indexes the full description, which repeats idle, tycoon, mining, planet, galaxy and empire naturally.
- **App Store keywords** don't repeat words from the name or subtitle, because Apple indexes those already. Words are comma-separated without spaces to fit more terms into 100 characters.
- **Competitors:** don't add other games' names to the keywords or description. Both stores reject listings that use other apps' trademarks.
- **Emojis** in the Play description are allowed and help scanning; the App Store keeps them too, but don't use them in the name or subtitle.
