Privacy Policy

Idle Space Miner by XeniumGames
Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Idle Space Miner (Android package com.xenium.idlespaceminer, "the Game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is published by XeniumGames ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The Game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads and Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent before these services use your data for personalised ads or analytics, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy settings.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (planets, resources, upgrades, settings and similar) and simple play statistics such as total play time and number of sessions are saved locally on your device. We do not upload this data to our own servers and we cannot access it. It is deleted when you use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the Game.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the following Google services, which collect some information automatically from your device. We do not receive information that directly identifies you.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example the Mine Boost or the Ark reward). To deliver and measure these ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA where permitted) and app set ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate (country or city level) location can be derived
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed, completed or tapped

Where required, ads are personalised only with your consent; otherwise non-personalised or limited ads are shown. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and How Google uses advertising data.

Google Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the Game, for example how long sessions last, how many players return, how far players progress, where they get stuck, and how the in-game economy is balanced. Firebase may collect:

  • An app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation) and, where permitted, the advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, country and app version
  • Usage events, such as sessions, engagement time, screens opened, game progress (for example planets bought, research completed, galaxies sold), in-game purchases made with in-game currency, and whether rewarded ads were watched

These events describe gameplay only; they do not contain your name, email address or other contact details. Learn more: Privacy and security in Firebase.

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

To ask for and remember your consent choices, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform. It stores your choices on your device (for example as an IAB TCF consent string) and may share them with AdMob and Firebase so they respect your choices.

4. Your consent and choices

  • Consent form: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent form before ads and analytics start. Until you consent, analytics storage and ad personalisation stay switched off.
  • Change your choice: open Settings > Privacy settings in the Game at any time to review or withdraw consent.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the Game stops all further collection.

5. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers listed above (Google LLC and its affiliates), which process it to provide their services to us and, for advertising, as independent controllers under Google's own policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. International transfers

Google may process information in the United States and other countries. Where data from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred, Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. Retention

Game progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the Game. Firebase Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 14 months and then deleted automatically. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold data that identifies you directly, the quickest way to exercise these rights is through the in-game Privacy settings and your device's ad settings. You can also contact us at the address below; to help us find data about your installation we may ask for your app instance ID. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help delete it.

10. Security

Information sent to Google's services is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work with providers that apply industry-standard safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game adds features or services. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Where required, we will ask for your consent again.
